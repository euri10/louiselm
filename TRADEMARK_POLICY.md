# Trademark policy

The LouiseLM name, logos, and associated marks identify this project. They are
reserved by the maintainer and are not licensed by the MIT grant for the
LouiseLM code. Permission to use the software does not include permission to
present another project, product, service, or organization as LouiseLM.

## Referring to LouiseLM

Anyone may use the name truthfully to refer to this project. Individuals,
non-profits, educational institutions, worker-owned cooperatives, and community
projects are especially welcome to discuss LouiseLM, teach with it, report on
it, and describe work built with it.

Forks and related projects may say that they are derived from, based on, or
compatible with LouiseLM. A modified project must use its own primary name and
visual identity, and must not imply that it is the LouiseLM project or is
endorsed by the maintainer. Nothing in this policy limits uses that applicable
trademark law permits without authorization.

## Uses that will be refused

No permission to use the LouiseLM marks as a brand, product identity, service
identity, or signal of endorsement will be granted to entities deriving a
majority of their revenue from proprietary foundation models, or from the
hardware those models run on.

This is a structural class, not a list of companies. Revenue is considered at
the level of the entity and any corporate group that controls it, so a rename,
successor, or subsidiary does not change the principle. The refusal concerns
the marks only. Entities in this class retain the same rights to use, modify,
and distribute the code that the software licence grants to everyone else, and
may still refer truthfully to LouiseLM.

## Other uses

Ask the maintainer before using a LouiseLM mark in a way not expressly covered
above, especially as the name of an event, publication, domain, product, or
service. Permission, when granted, applies only to the use described and does
not transfer ownership of the mark.
