# Political statement

LouiseLM is an anti-capitalist project. Its purpose is to give people more
control over the systems that turn their attention, judgment, and ideas into
software. It favors tools that are local-first, legible, and owned by their
operator over services that make productive work dependent on a vendor.

This statement is not a licence or a condition of use. It does not add to or
narrow the legal permissions granted with LouiseLM. The licence stands on its
own; this document explains the position behind choosing it.

## Reach is the mechanism

LouiseLM uses a permissive licence because its effect depends on reach. A
software harness matters politically when it is available everywhere and
shapes ordinary work: when people can inspect it, change it, run it on their
own machines, and decide which agents and services enter their workflow.
Friction works against that aim.

The licence therefore carries no political test. People and organizations do
not have to agree with this statement to use the code. Every installation that
moves control from a proprietary service toward its operator is a small
decommodification of the means of producing software. Making that move easy is
more valuable here than using copyright to select acceptable users.

## What remains reserved

A permissive copyright licence is not a surrender of every instrument. The
LouiseLM name and associated marks remain reserved. The licence contains no
explicit patent grant. The maintainer also retains the ability to release
future versions under different terms; versions already received under their
licence keep the rights that licence granted.

Those choices are deliberate. Trademark is the instrument available now for
deciding who may present themselves as LouiseLM. Patent rights, where they
exist, are one of the few instruments capable of reaching an independent
reimplementation rather than only copied source. Neither belongs silently
inside a promise made for the sake of distribution.

No permission to use the LouiseLM marks will be granted to entities deriving a
majority of their revenue from proprietary foundation models, or from the
hardware those models run on. That refusal applies to the name, not the code.
Such entities receive the same permissions under the software licence as
anyone else, but they may not present a fork, product, or service as LouiseLM.
The structural definition is intentional: it states a class position that
survives company renames, subsidiaries, and changes in the market.

## Community after code scarcity

Open-source communities have often formed because people needed one another's
code. AI is reducing that dependence: implementation that once required a
group can increasingly be produced by one person working with agents. That
does not make other people unnecessary. It changes what is scarce.

The contributions LouiseLM needs most are workflows, evidence, criticism, and
judgment: accounts of where attention was preserved or wasted, reproducible
failures, better boundaries, and decisions that withstand use. The current
[contribution policy](CONTRIBUTING.md) reflects that change. Outside code may
be studied as a proposal, but the maintainer decides what enters the project
and reimplements accepted ideas. Community here forms around shared decisions
and demonstrated outcomes, not around accumulating patches.

## The limit of a licence

No licence ends exploitation, prevents appropriation, or guarantees that a
tool will be used for emancipatory ends. MIT permits commercial use, including
use by organizations opposed to this project's politics. That is the cost of
choosing ubiquity as the immediate mechanism.

A licence can influence whether a tool spreads, who may capture value from it,
and which leverage its author keeps. It cannot substitute for ownership,
organization, or political action. LouiseLM keeps the name, any patent leverage
that may exist, and control over future releases while making the present code
easy to adopt. Claiming that this arrangement achieves more would be flattery;
pretending it is politically neutral would be dishonest.
